Starting a blog post is so awkward. I take so long figuring out how to word things and this indecision was preventing me from starting, so I am just to going to start with this: I love the farms of the bay area, specifically those above Berkeley, around Petaluma, Bodega Bay, Sonoma County, and those south of San Jose, near Gilroy and Watsonville. On a farm like Goldridge in Sebastopol, I can attend a cooking demonstration on making olive oil-based hollandaise, which includes a ride to their farmhouse on an adorable apple-themed car. At the Nest Box down in San Martin, I can play with the friendly farm cats, pigs, sheep, and buy eggs that are freshly "squeezed out" from the happy free-roaming chickens. Weekends spent exploring local farms make me feel like I'm "living off the fatta the lan'". And I feel much more connected to the epic farm fresh Californian food scene that started in Berkeley. 

This weekend, I visited the Sonoma Harvest Market and Cloverfield Organic Farm for the first time. The Harvest Market sells produce that is grown in a 6 acre plot of land run by volunteers and local residents. You can walk around the area and visit the different gardens. The sunflowers and zinnias looked amazing this time of year.

Cloverfield Organic Farm was just about the coolest experience I have had this year! It's a U-pick farm and they grow so many sorts of fruits and veggies, some of which I had never even heard of, like olive berries, which are a type of tart stone fruit, and alpine strawberries, which are cream-colored and taste of pineapple. To be honest, I actually don't really know what veggies look like when they're growing in the ground, so it was shocking and cathartic to pull a beet out of the ground. Twas an amazing experience and I will be back.
