Hello world, welcome to my blog!

I love to make things. As the owner of various (personal, cat, art, back-up art, cooking, photography) instagram accounts, I feel compelled to curate and share facets of my life with those I love. And I think this digital age is insane. Instagram, and ew, that other one have evolved past my threshold for stimulation. I cannot keep up with the reels and the deals. It's too hip happening! But I still have hobbies. I still yearn to create and grow and write silly little poems. When I started my PhD, I wanted to make gRad School Comics, be a science influencer, and start a dance flash mob. I'm 4 years in now and this website is my way of doing those things, just more softly.
